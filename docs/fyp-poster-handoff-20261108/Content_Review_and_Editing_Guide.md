## What “200 → 199” meant

The earlier version showed a local functional test: the example database started with 200 salad bowls; placing one eligible meal order deducted one bowl, leaving 199. Chicken went from 5,000 g to 4,870 g because the example recipe required 130 g. The purpose was to verify automatic ingredient and packaging deduction. These were controlled example records, not a survey statistic, a count of real customer orders or proof of actual physical stock.

The revised poster now says: “Ordering one meal deducted 130 g of chicken and one salad bowl.” This is one test example, not evidence that every scenario is defect-free. Formal client acceptance and student survey results remain uncollected. Do not insert invented ratings or percentages to fill that space.

## Editing without Codex

Download this ZIP to your laptop now. It contains ordinary files and does not need an active Codex subscription for later editing.

1. Open FYP_Poster_A1_Editable.pptx in PowerPoint, or import it into Canva. Keep a copy before editing.
2. The main poster contains separate text boxes, shapes and connectors. Reword, move, resize or remove sections as needed. Original objectives are also in Original_Objectives.txt for checking.
3. Optional_Poster_Text.docx contains short copyable sections, without invented evaluation findings.
4. Optional_Diagrams contains the current report diagrams as PNG, SVG and editable .drawio files. Use diagrams.net to edit .drawio files. Insert a single clear model instead of shrinking every diagram until labels cannot be read.
5. Source_Screenshots contains genuine app captures, including sales and menu suggestions if more screenshots are requested.
6. Keep the final poster at A1, 594 × 841 mm, unless instructed otherwise. Review line breaks, font substitutions, overlaps and QR readability after importing or exporting in another editor.
7. Export a new PDF and inspect it before printing. Preserve the original files as a recovery copy.

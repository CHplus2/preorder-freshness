Web-Based Preorder and Freshness Management System for Home-Based Entrepreneurs

---

The gap is the connection between tasks.
- Advance dates can require separate coordination.
- Ingredient shelf life is not part of the ordering tools reviewed.
- A shared marketplace limits independent storefront presentation.
- Sales information and menu choices need useful, integrated support.

---

The five original objectives
- 1. To design and develop a web application that supports flexible date-based food preordering and scheduling for home-based food entrepreneurs.
- 2. To implement a food stock freshness monitoring feature based on ingredient shelf life and storage duration.
- 3. To develop a personalised single-vendor storefront module that allows sellers to strengthen branding without direct competitor distraction.
- 4. To implement a sales analytics dashboard that supports data-driven business decision-making for home-based food operators.
- 5. To develop a menu recommendation system that reduces customer decision fatigue and improves purchase conversion rates.

---

Client feedback shaped the implementation.
- Iterative development: requirement → implementation → check → feedback.
- Name and phone details, with passkey access.
- Morning delivery, area fees and optional collection.
- Packaging stock, payment evidence and simpler receipt status.

One order connects the records.
- Choose food, date, area and payment method.
- Server checks price, offered days and recorded stock.
- Acceptance saves order details and deducts ingredients + packaging together.
- Owner reviews payment and actual food receipt separately.

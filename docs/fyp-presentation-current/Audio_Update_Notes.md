# Narration processing

Updated 10 November 2026 for the calmer walkthrough. Female narration uses Kokoro af_heart and male narration uses am_michael. Both are stock synthetic voices generated locally with Sherpa-ONNX.

The new script ends on daily printing and sales. Speech plays at its original generated pace. Each voice receives one constant volume adjustment, with no post-generation time stretching, compression or dynamic loudness processing. Audio stays at the model's native 24 kHz sample rate. Scene padding uses exact sample counts, and captions follow the revised narration.

Both MP4s have 120-second audio and video tracks. Full decoding, finite samples, true peak and clipping checks were run. These technical checks establish timing and basic signal integrity; they do not establish listener preference. Short female and male previews are included.

Earlier exports slowed generated narration to 90% or 82% speed. Extra time stretching can contribute to a rough or warbling sound; clipping was not established as the cause of the reported audio quality. The current export applies no such tempo filter.

The main PowerPoint files contain the matching updated videos. Their other package parts are preserved and were revalidated. Rehearse playback and volume on the presentation laptop.

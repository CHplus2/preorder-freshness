## Why the navbar looked narrower in the demo

The public header CSS gives its content roughly 1,240 px of width. At a 1,920 px viewport, that leaves about 340 px on each side; at 1,366 px, about 63 px. The content therefore occupies less of a wider screen even though the header background spans the page. The demo also fits the page at 90% inside a 1920 × 1080 frame to leave separate title/caption strips. That makes it look smaller again. The navbar was recorded from the real app, not drawn, and no website CSS was changed in this poster update.
